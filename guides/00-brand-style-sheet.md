# Brand Style Sheet — "The Ranking Board" look

Every template in the other guides pulls from this page. Decide once, never think about it again.
If you want different colors or fonts, change them HERE first, then rebuild the templates.

> This is an original look. Don't copy the show's logo, title font, or Showcase/Netflix branding. Fans should recognize the *vibe* (trailer park at night, cheap neon, liquor-store signage), not a knockoff of the official artwork.

## Colors (copy these hex codes into CapCut's color picker)

| Role | Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Asphalt | `#14161A` | Main background on every graphic |
| Panel | Trailer Siding | `#23262D` | Cards, board rows |
| Primary accent | Liquor-Store Neon Yellow | `#FFD23F` | Scores, rank numbers, highlights |
| Secondary accent | Cop-Car Red | `#E8463A` | Low scores, "worst" tier, warnings |
| Cool accent | Shitmobile Teal | `#3FB8AF` | Episode titles, labels |

| Muted text | Ashtray Grey | `#8B8F98` | Air dates, small labels, "IMDb" fan scores |

**Score colors (always the same so viewers learn them):**
- 40–50 → Neon Yellow `#FFD23F`
- 30–39 → Teal `#3FB8AF`
- 20–29 → Ashtray Grey `#8B8F98`
- under 20 → Cop-Car Red `#E8463A`

## Fonts

Pick ONE heading font and ONE body font and never change them.

- **Headings / numbers:** Bebas Neue or Anton (tall, condensed, loud). Free on Google Fonts.
- **Quotes / body:** Inter or Archivo (clean, readable on a phone).
- If CapCut doesn't show your installed font, use the closest bold condensed font in CapCut's own library and write its exact name here: `Heading font in CapCut: ________` `Body font in CapCut: ________`

## Sizes (1920×1080 long-form canvas)

- Rank numbers / big scores: 220–300
- Episode titles: 70–90
- Quote text: 60–72 (max 2 lines on screen)
- Labels (air date, "Season 3"): 36–44
- Keep everything 100 px away from every edge (YouTube's player bar and phone notches eat the edges).

For 9:16 Shorts (1080×1920): same fonts and colors, roughly the same sizes, and keep text out of the bottom 350 px and right 150 px (that's where YouTube puts the title, buttons and caption).

## Motion rules

- One "in" animation per element, 0.3–0.5 s. Don't stack effects.
- Scores count up or slam in — pick one and use it for the whole series.
- Every graphic holds still for at least 1.5 s after it lands so people can read it.

## Recurring series elements (consistency across season videos)

- **Intro sting:** 3–5 s, same every season video (built later in `guides/` when we do the first season video).
- **Ranking board:** same layout every season, only the rows change.
- **Outro card:** "Next: Season X+1" tease, same layout every time.
- Everything else (hooks, openings, structure, jokes) should **vary** video to video. Consistent *look*, different *content*. That's the line between a branded series and "mass-produced templated content."
